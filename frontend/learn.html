Glossary

SMILESA line notation that writes a molecule's atoms, bonds, branches and rings as a text string.
FragmentA small (< 300 Da) piece of a molecule that binds a target efficiently and can be grown into a lead.
TransformerA neural network that uses self-attention to model relationships between all tokens in a sequence.
TokenizerSplits text into vocabulary units (tokens). SmileBERTa uses a 767-token BPE vocabulary for SMILES.
DockingComputationally placing a ligand in a protein binding site and scoring how well it fits.
Binding affinityThe strength of ligand–protein binding, expressed as ΔG (kcal/mol) or Ki/Kd.
Force fieldA parameterised energy function (bonds, angles, torsions, non-bonded terms) used to model molecular geometry.
ConformerOne 3D shape of a molecule, reached by rotating single bonds without breaking any.
QEDQuantitative Estimate of Drug-likeness: a 0–1 score of how closely a molecule resembles approved oral drugs.
Lipinski's Rule of FiveMW ≤ 500, cLogP ≤ 5, ≤ 5 H-bond donors and ≤ 10 acceptors: a heuristic for oral bioavailability.
B-factorA crystallographic measure of how much each atom vibrates or is disordered. High values mean flexible regions.
Secondary structureLocal protein folding patterns: α-helices, β-sheets and the coils between them.
