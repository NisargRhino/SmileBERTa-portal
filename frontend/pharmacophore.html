Pharmacophore Predictor

For every heavy atom of a molecule, which protein interactions it is predicted to make — hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, π-stacking, hydrophobic contact. Trained on interactions measured in crystal structures, not on docking scores. The prediction labels atoms of the molecule you give it, so the answer is always a real substructure of your input.

Checking for a trained model…

Without a PDB entry the model answers which atoms of this molecule tend to interact in general. To ask which atoms bind a particular protein it needs the crystallised pose, so give an entry in which this molecule is bound.
