Pricing

SmileBERTa is free while it is in beta, and the free tier is built to stay useful rather than become a demo. The paid tiers below are where it is heading — they are not live yet, and there is nothing to buy on this page.

Everything is free today.

No card, no trial clock, no feature locked behind a paywall. We are in beta with a small group of researchers, and paid plans will only arrive once there is something worth charging for — dedicated compute and scale, not access to the science.

What we would actually charge for

The tools themselves are built on open science — RDKit, AutoDock Vina, published ADMET models — and it would be a strange thing to put a toll booth in front of work other people gave away. What genuinely costs money is compute: CPU hours for docking, capacity so your jobs do not queue behind everyone else's, and storage for results. That is what the paid tiers are for, and it is why the free tier can stay generous.

The one thing that is genuinely ours is the fragment-prediction model. When it is good enough to stand on published benchmark numbers, that is what will be worth paying for — and we would rather show you those numbers first.
