How to ask

Chain the work in one request — “dock both of these into 5IKR, then fragment whichever binds better”. Bert runs the steps in order, compares the numbers itself and carries the result forward.

What to trust

Every number Bert reports comes from a tool run in your conversation — he does not invent affinities or citations. Docking uses a rigid receptor and Vina's typical error is about 2 kcal/mol, so scores compare related compounds in the same pocket rather than measure them.
