Force fields and geometry optimisation

What happens when you press Optimise geometry in the Molecule Builder: molecular mechanics, the MMFF94 and UFF force fields, energy minimisation and conformer search.

Molecules as balls and springs

Quantum chemistry gives the most accurate molecular energies but is expensive. Molecular mechanics trades some accuracy for enormous speed: atoms are treated as balls, bonds as springs, and the energy of a geometry is a sum of simple, parameterised terms. A force field is that functional form plus thousands of parameters (ideal bond lengths, angles, force constants, atomic charges) fitted to experiments and quantum calculations.

Etotal = Ebond + Eangle + Etorsion + Eout-of-plane + Evan der Waals + Eelectrostatic

The first four are bonded terms between atoms connected by 1–3 bonds. The last two are non-bonded terms between atoms further apart, and they dominate how a molecule folds onto itself and how it interacts with a protein.

The energy terms

Bond stretching

Springs around an ideal length r₀. MMFF94 adds cubic and quartic corrections so very stretched bonds behave realistically.

E = ½·kb(r − r₀)²

Angle bending

Keeps bond angles near their ideal values, about 109.5° for sp³, 120° for sp² and 180° for sp atoms.

E = ½·kθ(θ − θ₀)²

Torsion

A periodic cosine series that sets rotational barriers, for example the staggered-versus-eclipsed preference of ethane.

E = ½[V₁(1+cos φ) + V₂(1−cos 2φ) + V₃(1+cos 3φ)]

Van der Waals

Strong repulsion at short range and weak dispersion attraction further out. MMFF94 uses a "buffered 14-7" form, softer than Lennard-Jones.

E = ε(1.07R*/(R+0.07R*))⁷ · (1.12R*⁷/(R⁷+0.12R*⁷) − 2)

Electrostatics uses Coulomb's law between partial atomic charges, screened with a distance-dependent dielectric to mimic solvent. Out-of-plane terms keep sp² centres such as carbonyl carbons and aromatic rings flat.

MMFF94, MMFF94s and UFF

Force fieldBest forNotes
MMFF94Drug-like organic moleculesMerck Molecular Force Field (Halgren, 1996). Parameterised against high-level quantum data for exactly the chemistry found in medicines. The default in the builder.
MMFF94sCrystal-like conformationsThe “static” variant: modified out-of-plane and torsion terms make delocalised nitrogens (anilines, amides) more planar, matching time-averaged crystal structures.
UFFAnything on the periodic tableUniversal Force Field (Rappé et al., 1992). Parameters come from element rules, so it handles metals and unusual atoms that MMFF lacks, but it is less accurate for organic molecules. The builder falls back to UFF automatically when MMFF has no parameters.
Energies from different force fields are on different scales and cannot be compared with each other. Even within one force field, only energy differences between conformations of the same molecule are meaningful.

Energy minimisation

A hand-built molecule usually has strained bond lengths and angles. Geometry optimisation moves the atoms downhill on the energy surface until the forces (the negative gradient of the energy) are close to zero. SmileBERTa runs RDKit's implementation, a quasi-Newton BFGS minimiser that builds an approximation of the energy surface's curvature as it goes, so it converges in far fewer steps than plain steepest descent.

Conformer search

To find low-energy shapes beyond the nearest minimum, the builder's Conformer search generates many diverse starting geometries with ETKDG v3, a distance-geometry method that adds torsion preferences learned from crystal structures. It minimises each one with MMFF94, removes duplicates (RMSD < 0.25 Å), and ranks the rest by energy relative to the best. Conformers within about 3 kcal/mol of the minimum are typically populated at room temperature, and the binding conformation is often among them.

What the builder does

  1. While you draw, hydrogens are added automatically to satisfy each atom's valence, and a lightweight in-browser spring model (bonds plus 1–3 angle springs plus soft repulsion) cleans the geometry instantly. It is not a real force field and only produces a sensible starting point.
  2. Optimise geometry sends the atoms and bonds to the server, which builds an RDKit molecule, sets up MMFF94, MMFF94s or UFF, and minimises it with BFGS. Snapshots are recorded along the way and played back in the viewer, with the energy trace plotted live.
  3. Search (ETKDG) runs the conformer search described above on the current molecule.
